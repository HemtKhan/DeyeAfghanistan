Questions

Deye Afghanistan — FAQ

What size Deye inverter do I need for my home?
It depends on your total appliance load and whether you want backup for the whole house or just essential circuits. Message us on WhatsApp with your appliance list and we'll recommend a size.
Do Deye inverters switch to battery automatically during a power outage?
Yes. Deye hybrid inverters detect a grid outage and switch to battery power within milliseconds, so most households don't notice the interruption.
Can a Deye inverter work without a battery?
Yes, a Deye hybrid inverter can run in grid-tied mode without a battery, but you won't have backup power during an outage. Most customers in Afghanistan add a lithium battery specifically for backup during load-shedding.
What warranty do you provide?
Every Deye inverter and battery we sell carries a 5-year warranty — up to 10 years on select battery models. Message us on WhatsApp for the exact terms on the model you're interested in.
Can I add solar panels later if I start with just a battery backup?
Yes. A Deye hybrid system can start as a battery-only backup charging from city power, and solar panels can be added later without replacing the inverter or battery.
